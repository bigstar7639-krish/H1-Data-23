Q+A Katha - Money 🧠

Question: What manufacturer made the car used in Back to the Future?

Answer: DeLorean - Vehicles. Itha kelvi Money pathi - Answer DeLorean. Money pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Money pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Money oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Money ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Money la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
Q+A Katha - Yoga 🧠

Question: Whose greyscale face is on the kappa emoticon on Twitch?

Answer: Josh DeSeno - General Knowledge. Itha kelvi Yoga pathi - Answer Josh DeSeno. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Yoga pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Yoga la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Yoga oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
